Low Poly Rats

Procedural & Animated

Coming to Unity Asset Store
Planned price $9.99 USD · all three variants

Actual Unity footage · Idle, slow walk, run, attack, hit and death. Left to right: Giant Rat, Cave Rat, Rat. Silent preview using the procedural prefabs.

From small scavenger to dungeon threat

A brown Rat, a larger black Cave Rat with emissive red eyes, and a Giant Rat with a metal harness and visible teeth.

Procedural prefabs adapt their footsteps to movement speed. Standard Generic Animator prefabs provide six in-place motions per variant: Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Hit and Death.

  • Three rigged models · 18 weighted bones each
  • Rat and Cave Rat: 1,812 triangles each
  • Giant Rat: 2,712 triangles
  • Six visual prefabs: three procedural, three Animator
  • Editable FBX models, animation clips and controllers
  • One URP material per variant · nine 128 × 16 maps total
  • Interactive demo and English offline documentation

The 18 animation takes are six shared motions adapted to the three variants.

Coming soon to Unity Asset Store

Compatibility

Tested with Unity 6000.3.23f1 and URP 17.3.0. Requires URP; Built-in and HDRP materials are not included.

Know before you build

Visual creature pack: connect your own controller and gameplay. Procedural feet use a flat reference plane, with no terrain raycast IK. Clips are in-place, with no dedicated backward or strafe motions. Navigation, combat, sounds, character colliders, ragdolls and LODs are not included. An eye bloom halo requires scene post-processing.

Low Poly Dwarven Mine Kit

A worksite beneath the mountain

In preparation for Unity Asset Store

Timber, stone and forged iron

Build a stylized fantasy mine from a matching set of carts, modular rail sections, resource deposits and architectural props.

  • 27 main models and prefabs, including cart-load and gate variants
  • Editable FBX models and shared URP materials
  • Mine diorama and complete prefab overview scenes
  • Lantern and forge lights with optional subtle flicker
  • English offline documentation and technical inventory

Static art package. No mining gameplay, train simulation or rail-drawing tool is included. The two mine facades are decorative, with no terrain cutting or level-transition system.

Compatibility

Prepared for Unity 6000.3.23f1 and URP 17.3.0. Built-in and HDRP materials are not included. Visible bloom requires camera post-processing.
