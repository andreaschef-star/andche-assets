Ready to open, close and lock.

Connect the included DoorMotion component to your interaction system. Reverse a moving door, set its lock state and respond to open/closed events. The frame stays fixed while each leaf and its collision move together.

24-second silent demonstration of the actual Unity prefabs. Opening uses scripted kinematic movement, not Animator clips.

Twelve designs. One coordinated collection.

Interior & front doors

Flush Interior, Panel Interior, Frosted Interior, Timber Front and Glazed Front. Distinct panels, mouldings, handles and original oak/walnut grain textures.

Shop & service entrances

Steel Service, Fire Exit and Double Shop. Ventilation grilles, panic-bar hardware and a coordinated pair of opening glass leaves.

Sliding doors

Glass Sliding and Industrial Sliding. One glass panel slides past its fixed neighbour; the industrial door travels along its overhead rail.

Access hatches

Floor Hatch and Access Hatch. Chequered tread on the floor cover and a compact wall-access design, both with working pivots and collision.

Inspect the details before you integrate.

The included showroom lets you select and isolate a model, orbit and zoom, open or close it, toggle the lock and cycle its motion. Editable Blender source and English setup/API documentation are included.

Built for Unity URP.

Compatibility

Tested in Unity 6000.3.23f1 (6.3 LTS), Universal RP 17.3.0 on macOS Apple Silicon. Input System 1.20.0 powers demo shortcuts. DoorMotion itself has no Input System dependency.

Geometry & materials

12 prefabs, 36 FBX files and three configured LODs per model. LOD0: 4,752–13,088 triangles per design; 104,492 in total. Metre scale, +Z prefab fronts, tiled primary UVs and secondary lightmap UVs. URP materials, two original 1024 × 1024 wood albedo maps and twelve 1024 × 128 demo labels. Compound box colliders are included.

Integration

Open, close, reverse and lock through the supplied C# API. Add your own player interaction, navigation, networking and save logic.

Know before you build

URP only; Built-in and HDRP materials are not included. No Animator clips, audio, physics hinges or obstruction safety. Glass does not refract or blur the background. The floor hatch needs a pre-existing floor opening and includes no ladder or terrain cutting. Other platforms have not been validated.

Frequently asked questions

Are these static props?

The frames are static, but the door leaves and hatch covers open and close using the supplied DoorMotion script. Their colliders follow the motion. Your game supplies the interaction trigger.

Can I edit the models?

Yes. The package includes editable FBX files and a Blender source archive. Each design has three LOD levels.

Is this part of Project Aether?

No. This is an original standalone product built and tested in separate projects.

Can I buy it now?

Not yet. The Asset Store listing is a draft. The planned base price is $15 USD before taxes; availability will be updated here after release.
