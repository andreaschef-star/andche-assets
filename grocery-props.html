What the current build includes.

Technical scope

Unity 6000.3.23f1 / URP 17.3.0. FBX meshes, a shared 4096 × 4096 colour atlas, shared URP materials, 256 × 256 transparent inventory sprites and simple colliders. Real-world metre scale and bottom-centred pivots.

Know before you build

Static props only. No checkout, inventory, pickup or economy gameplay, sounds, animations or LODs. Built-in and HDRP materials are not included. Bottles and jars use opaque stylized surfaces. Product colliders approximate their outer shape, including open variants; physics-based filling needs custom colliders. Final release validation is still pending.

AI-assisted modeling scripts, original packaging artwork and example code, followed by inspection and iteration. Images show the actual Unity models. No sales or popularity claims are implied by this development preview.
