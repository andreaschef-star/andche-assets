Technical scope

Unity 6000.3.23f1 / URP 17.3.0. 60 editable FBX models, one shared 4096 × 4096 colour atlas, a 512 × 512 surface mask, shared URP materials and 60 transparent 256 × 256 inventory sprites. Metre scale, bottom placement pivots and +Z fronts. 132–2,596 triangles per model; 57,896 across all 60. English documentation and a per-model inventory are included.

Know before you build

Static visual props: no checkout, inventory, pickup or economy gameplay, audio, animations or LODs. URP is required; Built-in and HDRP materials are not included. Bottles and jars use opaque stylized surfaces. Compound colliders preserve open container, basket and crate cavities; lattice holes are not individually simulated. Add your own Rigidbody and interaction components. Fixture prices are decorative.

AI-assisted procedural modeling, original fictional packaging and demonstration code, followed by manual direction, inspection and technical validation. Images show the actual Unity models. No external model pack is required.
